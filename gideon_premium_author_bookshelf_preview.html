About Gideon

A Christian author, Bible teacher and minister of the Gospel with a passion for helping people grow deeper in their relationship with God.

Gideon Sintim Aboagye writes and teaches on prayer, spiritual growth, wisdom, purpose, the anointing, holiness, maturity and victorious Christian living.

The goal is not simply to help people know about God, but to encourage a life of deeper prayer, biblical understanding, spiritual maturity and purposeful service.

Core Transformation

Know God. Grow in Him. Discover your purpose. Walk it out.

The books and ministry are designed to help believers move from information to transformation.
